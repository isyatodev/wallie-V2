        Heads up: the dashboard runs on 127.0.0.1 by default
        and is unauthenticated. If you change DASHBOARD_HOST to bind
        to your LAN, anyone on that network can read masked previews and
        overwrite your keys. Use a reverse proxy with auth in that case.
